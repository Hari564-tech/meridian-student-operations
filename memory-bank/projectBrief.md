# Abroad Aspirants — Student Operations Platform

## Project Overview
Abroad Aspirants (formerly Meridian) is an enterprise student operations and admissions management web application. It enables educational counselors, administrators, and students to track university applications, financial records, visa requirements, country-specific workflows (notably Germany APS & Uni-Assist), and document lifecycles.

## Core Features & Objectives
1. **Brand Identity & Theming**:
   - Company branding: "Abroad Aspirants" with official framer-hosted SVG logo and modern design tokens.
   - Comprehensive multi-role portal (Admin, Counselor/Employee, Student upload portal).
2. **Student Onboarding & Registration**:
   - Multi-step registration workflow (Personal, Academic, Destination & Intake, Germany-specific credentials, Review).
   - German-specific details: APS Username/Password, Uni-Assist ID/Password, Blocked Account, Enrollment Status, Student Dorm Status.
3. **Student Profile & Document Operations**:
   - Prominent Document Submission Status card near top of profile and in profile header.
   - Interactive, in-place Germany-Specific Status management with independent dropdowns, semantic status badges, and instant audit logging.
   - Category-organized "Download All Documents (.ZIP)" with `Document_Manifest.txt`, file sanitization, progress tracking dialog, and export audit event dispatch.
   - University shortlist, eligibility checking, and downloadable eligibility receipt with company branding.
